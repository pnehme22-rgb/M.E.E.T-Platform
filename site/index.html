Mind Exploring Every Tradition

Bring any creative vision closer to reality.

M.E.E.T. brings together artists of different practices, materials and traditions, and the people who want to work with them. Discover an artist, commission a piece, or start a project with the help of our team.

For artists

A place for your practice, and what it could become.

Present your work in a reviewed profile where collectors, designers and organizations can find it. People can write to you about a piece or a commission, and the M.E.E.T. team can invite you into projects alongside artists from other disciplines.

Collaboration

What becomes possible when artistic worlds meet.

A weaver and a ceramicist. A calligrapher and a glassmaker. Choose the artists whose practices you would like to bring together and describe your idea. The M.E.E.T. team contacts them, checks what is possible, and comes back to you with a proposal.
