Introducing M.E.E.T.

Where artists, ideas and cultures meet.

Every artist has a practice shaped by experience, knowledge, culture and a unique way of seeing the world.

M.E.E.T. stands for Mind Exploring Every Tradition. We are building a place where those practices can be seen side by side: a potter in one city can find a weaver in another, and the people who love their work can find them both.

Every encounter holds the possibility of something new.

  • 01

    Connect

    Artists meeting artists, and the people looking for their work.

  • 02

    Preserve

    Visibility for traditional craft, cultural knowledge and the hands that carry it forward.

  • 03

    Experience

    Creative exchanges that go beyond the screen, and one day, hands-on time with the makers themselves.

Two sides of one visionWhat artists can discover through M.E.E.T.

M.E.E.T. is being built around two ideas: helping artists reach the people who value their work, and helping artists find one another across materials, traditions and borders.

Both begin with the community that forms now. The artists who join early help shape what the platform becomes: which connections matter most, which practices deserve more visibility, and which kinds of creative exchange are worth building.

01 Artists and opportunities

Let your work find its people.

Art creates connections between the people who make it and the people who connect with it. M.E.E.T. aims to help artists reach new audiences, meet potential clients and find opportunities for their work to travel further.

Work is made to be seen, often by people the artist has not met yet.

02 Artists and collaboration

Discover what happens when practices meet.

Different materials, traditions and ways of thinking can open unexpected doors. M.E.E.T. aims to bring artists together to exchange knowledge, learn from one another and explore what they might create together.

M.E.E.T. is still being built. Joining now means you are among the first artists we contact as it takes shape. It does not guarantee sales, commissions or collaborations.

Every discipline is welcome

Ceramics, textiles, woodwork, glass, metal, calligraphy, painting, photography, sculpture, digital art, and the practice that is not on this list yet.

If you make something with skill, knowledge and intention, we would like to hear about it.
