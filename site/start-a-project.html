Start a Project

Larger work, carefully coordinated.

For interior designers, curators, businesses and organizations. Send a brief, and the M.E.E.T. team will find the artists, check what is possible and come back to you with a tailored proposal.

Write your brief

How we help

From brief to confirmed project.

  1. Your brief

    The space, the purpose, the disciplines or materials, your inspiration, budget and timeline. You can name artists you would like involved.

  2. Artists and feasibility

    The team contacts the artists you named and others who could suit the project, and checks availability, pricing and timelines.

  3. A tailored proposal

    The creative approach, the artists and their contributions, estimated costs and the timeline.

  4. Refine, then confirm

    Discuss it, ask for changes or alternatives. Nothing proceeds until you accept the proposal.
