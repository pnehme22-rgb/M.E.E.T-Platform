For artists

A place for your practice, and what it could become.

M.E.E.T. presents your work to collectors, designers and organizations looking for it, and makes room for commissions and collaborations with artists from other disciplines and traditions.

Apply to M.E.E.T.How it works

What M.E.E.T. offers artists

  • Be discovered

    A reviewed profile where people can explore your practice, your materials and techniques, and every artwork you choose to present.

  • Explore new possibilities

    Receive inquiries about your work and commissions, and be considered for projects that bring artists together across disciplines, materials and traditions.

  • Find support and connection

    Join an international artistic network. When you ask for help, or when a client brings a project to M.E.E.T., the team works on it with you.

How it works

Three steps, at your pace.

  1. Apply

    Tell us about your practice in a short application. It takes a few minutes and needs no account.

  2. Review

    The M.E.E.T. team reviews your application and your work, and may ask you a question.

  3. Be presented

    Once approved, you complete your studio. Your profile and each artwork become visible to visitors after the team has reviewed them.

Every application is read by a person, and approval is not automatic. Being part of M.E.E.T. does not guarantee sales or commissions. Inquiries come to you directly; the team steps in when you ask for help, or when a client brings a project to M.E.E.T.

Your practice belongs here

Show the world what you make.

Ceramics, textiles, wood, glass, metal, calligraphy, painting, photography and every practice in between. If you make something with skill and intention, we would like to see it.
