Collaboration

What becomes possible when artistic worlds meet.

Choose the artists you would like to bring together and describe your idea. Your request comes to the M.E.E.T. team, not directly to the artists.

How it works

We coordinate it with you.

  1. You choose and describe

    Select the artists and share your idea, inspiration, timing and budget.

  2. We contact the artists

    The team checks their availability, pricing, timelines and what is feasible.

  3. You receive a proposal

    The creative direction, each artist's contribution, the estimated budget and timeline. If an artist is unavailable, we suggest alternatives.

  4. You decide

    Discuss and refine the proposal. Nothing goes ahead until you accept it.
