Privacy notice

How we look after your application

Version 2026-10-20. This notice covers the M.E.E.T. artist registration form only.

What we collect

When you register, we store what you type into the form: your full name, professional or artist name, email address, country and city, your disciplines and description of your practice, any links you share, the interests you tick, where you found M.E.E.T. if you tell us, and the date you gave consent.

The registration form asks for no uploads, passwords or payment details. This site uses no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics.

Why we collect it

  • To review your application to the M.E.E.T. artist community.
  • To contact you about your application and about M.E.E.T. as it develops.
  • To understand which disciplines, places and interests are represented, so we can shape the platform around real artists.

We use your information on the basis of the consent you give in the form. You can withdraw it at any time.

Who can see it

Only the M.E.E.T. team, through a password-protected review area. We do not publish your application, sell it or share it with third parties for marketing. Your application itself is never published. Only the studio profile and artworks you add, once approved, appear publicly.

Applications are stored in a private database run for us by Supabase, on servers in the European Union. The website itself is hosted by Netlify. The M.E.E.T. team also keeps a working copy of applications in a private Google Sheet that only the team can open. Emails from M.E.E.T., such as your welcome email, are delivered by Resend.

If your application is approved

We create a M.E.E.T. studio for you and send you an email to set your password. When the M.E.E.T. team approves your profile and your artworks, they become public on M.E.E.T.: your artist name, city and country, disciplines, techniques, biography, pictures and approved artworks. Your legal name, email address, social media and website links are never shown publicly. Drafts and anything not approved stay private.

How long we keep it

We keep applications while M.E.E.T. is being developed and for up to 24 months after, unless you ask us to delete yours sooner.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your application, or withdraw your consent, at any time. Please write to minds.exploring.traditions@gmail.com and include your reference if you have it. We will reply within 30 days.

Registering is not a contract

Registering tells us you are interested. It does not guarantee acceptance, paid work, commissions or a collaboration.
