Privacy notice

How we look after your information

Version 2026-10-20, with the member accounts section added on 2026-10-24. This notice covers the M.E.E.T. artist registration form, artist studios and member accounts.

What we collect when artists apply

When you apply, we store what you type into the form: your full name, professional or artist name, email address, country and city, your disciplines and description of your practice, any links you share, the interests you tick, where you found M.E.E.T. if you tell us, and the date you gave consent.

The registration form asks for no uploads, passwords or payment details. This site uses no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics.

Why we collect it

  • To review your application to the M.E.E.T. artist community.
  • To contact you about your application and about M.E.E.T. as it develops.
  • To understand which disciplines, places and interests are represented, so we can shape the platform around real artists.

We use your information on the basis of the consent you give in the form. You can withdraw it at any time.

Who can see it

Only the M.E.E.T. team, through a password-protected review area. We never publish your application, sell it or share it with third parties for marketing. Only the studio profile and artworks you add, once approved, appear publicly.

Applications are stored in a private database run for us by Supabase, on servers in the European Union. The website itself is hosted by Netlify. The M.E.E.T. team also keeps a working copy of applications in a private Google Sheet that only the team can open. Emails from M.E.E.T., such as your welcome email, are delivered by Resend.

If your application is approved

We create a M.E.E.T. studio for you and send you an email to set your password. When the M.E.E.T. team approves your profile and your artworks, they become public on M.E.E.T.: your artist name, city and country, disciplines, techniques, biography, pictures and approved artworks, with the price of an artwork only if you choose to show it. Your legal name, email address, social media and website links are never shown publicly. Drafts and anything not approved stay private.

If you create a member account

When you create a Personal, Professional or Business & Organization account, we store the details you enter: your full name, email address, country, phone number if you give it, your password in encrypted form, and, depending on the account type, your professional role, company or studio, business or organization name, business type, contact person and website. We use them to run your account and to contact you about it. Only you and the M.E.E.T. team can see them, and they are not shown publicly.

When you send an inquiry to an artist, the artist sees your name, account type and country, with your message. Your email address and phone number are not shared with the artist. When you send a project request, the M.E.E.T. team uses your details to discuss the project with you.

You can change your details at any time in your account, and ask us to delete your account from the same page. We complete deletion requests within 30 days.

How long we keep it

We keep applications while M.E.E.T. is being developed and for up to 24 months after, unless you ask us to delete yours sooner.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your application, or withdraw your consent, at any time. Please write to minds.exploring.traditions@gmail.com and include your reference if you have it. We will reply within 30 days.

Applying is not a contract

Applying tells us you are interested. It does not guarantee acceptance, paid work, commissions or a collaboration.
