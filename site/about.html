About M.E.E.T.

Mind Exploring Every Tradition.

M.E.E.T. is an international platform for discovering artists, commissioning their work, and bringing different artistic practices together on a shared project.

The idea

Every practice carries a way of seeing.

A potter knows how clay behaves in the heat of a kiln. A weaver reads pattern in tension and colour. A calligrapher hears rhythm in a single line. Each practice holds knowledge built over years, often over generations, and shaped by the place and tradition it comes from.

M.E.E.T. starts from a simple belief: that knowledge deserves to be seen, and it becomes even more interesting when it meets another.

Why it is needed

Good work is often hard to find.

Many artists are discovered only through social media, where visibility depends on posting often and pleasing an algorithm. People looking for a particular skill, material or tradition rarely know where to begin, and bringing two or three practices together on one piece is harder still.

M.E.E.T. offers a more considered route: reviewed profiles that present each practice properly, and a team that can help when a project needs it.

The encounter

What becomes possible when artistic worlds meet.

Glass shaped around a woven form. Calligraphy carved into wood. A textile dyed with a technique from one country and stitched with a pattern from another. When different practices work together, they can create something neither would have reached alone.

That meeting is the heart of M.E.E.T., and the reason for its name.

In practice

How M.E.E.T. works.

  1. Discover

    Browse artists by discipline, material, technique and place. Every artist and every artwork is reviewed by the M.E.E.T. team before it appears.

  2. Write to an artist

    With a free account you can send an inquiry about a work or a commission directly to the artist.

  3. Ask the team

    Send a commission idea or a project brief. The team helps you find suitable artists, checks their availability and pricing, and prepares a proposal.

  4. Bring artists together

    Choose the artists you would like to combine. The team contacts them, explores what is possible and comes back to you with options.

M.E.E.T. is new and its community is growing. Every artist is reviewed before they appear, and joining does not guarantee commissions.
