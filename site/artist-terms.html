Artist terms

Your work, your rights

Version 2026-10-24. These terms apply when you use your M.E.E.T. studio.

Your work stays yours

You keep the copyright and every other right in your artworks, photographs and texts. Nothing here transfers ownership to M.E.E.T.

What you allow M.E.E.T. to do

When the M.E.E.T. team approves your profile or an artwork, you allow M.E.E.T. to:

  • show it publicly on the M.E.E.T. website and platform, with your name as the artist;
  • use it to present and promote M.E.E.T., for example on social media, in newsletters, presentations and printed materials, always credited to you;
  • resize or crop the photographs so they display well, without changing the work itself.

This permission is free of charge, not exclusive, and worldwide. M.E.E.T. will never sell your images, license them to others, or use them to train AI systems.

What becomes public, and when

Your profile becomes public on M.E.E.T. as soon as the team approves it. Each artwork becomes public as soon as the team approves that artwork. Drafts, items waiting for review and anything not approved stay private.

Your public profile shows your artist name, city and country, disciplines, techniques, biography and pictures. Your legal name, email address, social media and website links stay private to the M.E.E.T. team.

If you change something that is already public, it is hidden again until the team has reviewed the change.

You can change your mind

You can remove an artwork that is not yet approved at any time in your studio, and you can ask us to remove anything else, or your whole studio, by writing to minds.exploring.traditions@gmail.com. We remove it from the website and platform within 30 days. Printed materials that were already produced cannot be recalled.

What you confirm

  • The works you add are your own, or made with collaborators who agree to them being shown.
  • You have the right to share the photographs you upload.
  • Your descriptions, prices and details are honest.

What M.E.E.T. does not promise

Being part of M.E.E.T. does not guarantee sales, commissions, exhibitions or collaborations. Any future sale or commission through M.E.E.T. will follow separate terms that we will share and agree with you first.

Your personal details

How we store and protect your personal information is explained in the privacy notice.
