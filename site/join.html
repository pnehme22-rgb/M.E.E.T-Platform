Join the M.E.E.T. Artist Community

A few short questions about you and your practice. No account or password needed, and it only takes a few minutes.

What happens next

  1. The M.E.E.T. team reviews every application and may write to you with a question.
  2. If your application is approved, we email you a link to open your artist studio.
  3. In your studio you add your profile and artworks. Each one appears on M.E.E.T. after the team has reviewed it.

Applying does not guarantee acceptance, sales, commissions or collaborations.
