# Launch readiness: open items (24 October 2026)

These need a decision or qualified review before a wider public launch. Nothing here is treated as legally approved.

## Legal review
- **Artist terms** (`/artist-terms`, version 2026-10-24): the worldwide, free, non-exclusive promotional permission; the statement that M.E.E.T. never uses images to train AI; the 30-day removal period; the printed-materials exception; the no-guarantee-of-sales wording.
- **Privacy notice** (`/privacy`): now also describes member accounts, inquiries (what the artist sees) and project requests.
- **Member Terms of Use:** members (Personal, Professional, Business & Organization) have no Terms of Use yet. To be written in the account-registration phase and reviewed by someone with appropriate legal knowledge.
- **Public prices:** artists can now choose to show a price publicly. The current Artist terms do not mention public prices; consider covering this in the next terms version.
- **Deleted member accounts:** project records keep the organization, country and figures, with the person's name replaced by "Deleted member". Confirm this retention fits the privacy notice and applicable law.

## Product decisions
- **Cultural traditions** are stored privately and not shown or used for matching. They can become public only after the Artist terms cover them, are reviewed, and affected artists agree again.
- **Inquiry replies:** artists reply once through M.E.E.T.; there is no ongoing messaging. An artist may choose to include their own contact details in a reply.
