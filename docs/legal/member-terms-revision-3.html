1. Audit summary

The previous draft (revision 2) was sound in structure and fair in tone. Its main weaknesses were:

  1. Founder instructions inside member-facing clauses (3.1, 7.6, 9.2, 10.2, 11.1–11.3, 13.1, 14.3, 15.2, 17.1). All moved to this annex; only bracketed placeholders remain in the text.
  2. Closure versus deletion was described as "the current implementation", leaving it unclear what closure does to access, content and records. Section 9.2 now defines closure as a permanent decision carried out through the section 11 deletion process, with identical effects.
  3. Visitors were said to be bound by "certain provisions", which overstates contractual reach. Section 1.3 now says the Terms are not a contract with visitors and relies on intellectual-property law and the Privacy Notice.
  4. Indemnity said a Member "may be responsible", which is neither an obligation nor clearly limited. Section 15 is now a real, narrow indemnity for business use only, with a carve-out for M.E.E.T.'s own conduct and a claim-handling procedure.
  5. "Separate Agreement" was defined as a written agreement but used for direct Member–Artist deals that may not be written. It is split into Artist Agreement and Service Agreement, and section 19.1 sets out which document governs what.
  6. Deletion and retention were described generally. Section 11 now lists exactly what the code deletes and keeps, says retained project records may still identify the person, and states that the Team receives email copies and exports.
  7. Liability had no operative limit clause. Section 14.3 now contains a foreseeability limit and a placeholder cap.

Privacy Notice (version 2026-10-20, member section 2026-10-24)

  • Conflict: it says members can change their details "at any time in your account". Email and account type cannot be changed there (Terms 3.3).
  • Gap: "How long we keep it" covers applications only. Nothing on member accounts, retained Project Request records after deletion, the deletion log, Team mailbox copies or exports (Terms 11.3–11.4).
  • Gap: it names Supabase, Netlify, Google Sheets and Resend but not Cloudflare (bot protection), ImprovMX (email forwarding) or Gmail (Team mailbox for copies and exports). Terms 10.2 refers to providers "listed in the Privacy Notice", so this must be fixed before adoption.
  • Gap: it does not say the Team receives email copies of Inquiries, or that the Artist receives the Inquiry by email.
  • Gap: lawful basis is stated only for applications (consent); none is stated for member accounts.

Artist Terms (version 2026-10-24)

  • Consistent: artists keep their rights; M.E.E.T. will not use their images for AI training (Member Terms 7.3 prohibits Members from doing so); future sales or commissions "through M.E.E.T." follow separate terms agreed first (matches 5.6 and 6.4); no guarantee of sales.
  • Gap: no obligation on Artists to use a Member's name, country and message only to respond to the Inquiry, or to treat it confidentially.
  • Gap: they do not mention optional public prices (the Privacy Notice does).
  • Note: they allow removal of an artwork within 30 days on request; Member Terms make no promise that content stays available, so no conflict.

5. Prioritized final checklist

1. Required before the Terms take effect

  • Update the Privacy Notice for the gaps in section 4 (providers, member retention, email copies, lawful basis, editable fields).
  • Set version number and effective date and publish both documents together.
  • Ask existing Members to accept the Terms before they continue to send Inquiries or Project Requests.

2. Dependent on incorporation or jurisdiction

  • Insert legal name, legal form, registration details and address (1.1).
  • Choose governing law and dispute forum (16.2, 16.3) and the non-excludable liability wording (14.1).
  • Set up a monitored legal notices and IP complaints address (7.6, 18.2).

3. Founder business decisions

  • Minimum age (3.1).
  • Liability limit amount (14.3).
  • Retention periods for project records, deletion records, Team mailbox copies and exports (11.3, 11.4).
  • Whether deletion should also clear organization and free-text project fields.
  • Whether suspension should be built (optional).

4. Technical work

  • Build the acceptance step: show the Terms at sign-up, record version and timestamp, and prompt existing Members.
  • Add the age check once decided.
  • Add a Team tool, or a written procedure, for changing a Member's email and account type.
  • Drop the member's name from the deletion-request activity log entry, or document why it is kept.
  • Implement the chosen retention periods (including clearing old export emails) and a 30-day reminder for pending deletion requests.
  • Confirm whether a single Inquiry can be removed by the Team; if not, narrow 9.1.
  • Check the Supabase backup retention for the current plan.

5. Qualified legal review

  • Review the Member Terms, Artist Terms and Privacy Notice together.
  • Confirm the indemnity (15), liability limit (14.3), visitor approach (1.3) and whether a visitor notice is needed.
  • Confirm data-protection obligations in the chosen jurisdiction, Lebanon (Law 81/2018), the UAE (Decree-Law 14/2023) and, if Members are targeted in the EU, the GDPR, including whether retained project records count as personal data.
