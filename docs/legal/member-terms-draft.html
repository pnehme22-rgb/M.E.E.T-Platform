C. Legal review questions

Jurisdiction-dependent legal questions

  1. Which law should govern the Terms, given incorporation in an undecided country and Members in Lebanon, the UAE and elsewhere? Which mandatory consumer rules apply regardless of the chosen law?
  2. Does the UAE Decree-Law No. 14 of 2023 apply to a platform that serves UAE users but sells nothing and takes no payments? If so, what disclosures, licensing or security obligations follow?
  3. Under Lebanon's Law No. 81/2018, what is the lawful basis for each processing purpose, is any declaration or licence required, and what consent wording is needed at sign-up?
  4. Is M.E.E.T. subject to the GDPR under Article 3(2) for Members in the EU? Does hosting data with an EU-based provider change the analysis? Is an EU representative needed?
  5. What dispute-resolution clause is appropriate and enforceable, including for consumers and low-value disputes?
  6. What liability exclusions, cap and indemnity are enforceable against consumers versus business Members?
  7. What retention periods are justified for project records, record exports, email copies and deletion logs, and is replacing only the name in project records sufficient?
  8. Is the infringement-complaint procedure in 7.6 adequate, or does any jurisdiction require a specific notice-and-takedown process?
  9. How should M.E.E.T. describe its role when it introduces Members to Artists without being a party, so that it neither overstates nor understates responsibility?

Business decisions

  1. Minimum age for Member Accounts.
  2. Fee model (connection fee, service fee, commission): when it starts, and the separate agreement template that will disclose it.
  3. Liability cap amount and whether paid services will have their own terms.
  4. Which address receives legal notices and infringement complaints.
  5. Whether the M.E.E.T. Team should be able to restrict an account temporarily, rather than only delete it.

Technical implementation gaps

  1. Build the acceptance step for Members: show the Terms, record version and timestamp, and ask existing Members to accept before the Terms take effect.
  2. Add the age check once the policy is decided.
  3. Give the M.E.E.T. Team a way to change a Member's email address and account type on request (today this needs direct database work).
  4. Remove or anonymize the Member's name in the activity log entry created when they request deletion.
  5. Decide whether deletion should also clear organization name and free-text fields in project records that may identify the person.
  6. Define how long weekly export emails are kept in founders' mailboxes, and delete older ones accordingly.
