2Your account

  • You can create a Personal, Professional, or Business & Organization account. Each account belongs to one person, who must confirm their email address before signing in.
  • Give accurate details, including your country. Keep your password private; you are responsible for activity on your account.
  • If you create a Business & Organization account, you confirm that you may act for that organization.
  • The account type cannot be changed in the account itself. To change it, or your email address, write to us.

For review: the website does not currently ask for a minimum age. Decide whether accounts are for people aged 18 and over, and we will add the check.

3What you can do on M.E.E.T.

  • Browse artists and artworks the M.E.E.T. team has reviewed and approved. Browsing does not need an account.
  • Send inquiries to artists (section 4).
  • With a Professional or Business & Organization account, send project requests to the M.E.E.T. team (section 5).

4Inquiries to artists

  • When you send an inquiry, the artist sees your name, account type and country, together with your message. Your email address and phone number are not shared with the artist.
  • The artist can reply once through M.E.E.T. We email you the reply and show it in your account. An artist may choose to include their own contact details in a reply.
  • The M.E.E.T. team can see inquiries and their status, to follow and support them.
  • Artists reply when they can. A reply is not guaranteed.
  • To prevent misuse, the number of inquiries you can send in a day is limited.

For review: M.E.E.T. is not a party to any agreement you make directly with an artist. Confirm how to word this, and what M.E.E.T. is responsible for when an introduction leads to a sale or commission.

5Project requests

  • A project request describes artwork you are looking for (for example for a hotel, an office or a home). The M.E.E.T. team reviews it and may contact you by email.
  • Sending a request does not create an order or an obligation for you, for M.E.E.T., or for any artist. A project only goes ahead under a separate written agreement.
  • In your account you see the status of your requests: Received, In discussion, Confirmed, or Closed.
  • The team's internal notes and any artists it considers for your project are not shared with you automatically.

6No sales or payments on the website

The website does not sell artworks or take payments. Any sale, commission, or service by M.E.E.T. is agreed separately, in writing, before it starts, including any fee.

For review: M.E.E.T. may charge a connection fee when a client finds an artist on their own, and a separately agreed service fee or commission when M.E.E.T. sources or coordinates a project. Amounts and terms are not decided. Decide whether these terms should mention fees at all before they are final.

7Artworks and content

  • Artists keep the copyright in their artworks, photographs and texts. You may view them on M.E.E.T. for your own interest and for considering a purchase or project.
  • Do not copy, download in bulk, republish, or use them to train AI systems without the artist's permission.
  • A price appears only when the artist chose to show it. Prices and availability can change, and showing a price is not an offer to sell.

8Acceptable use

Do not use M.E.E.T. to:

  • send spam, advertising, or messages unrelated to an artist's work;
  • harass, threaten, or mislead artists, members, or the team;
  • give false information or pretend to be someone else;
  • collect artists' or members' information, or access the website with automated tools;
  • try to get around the website's security, including its bot protection.

How we collect, use and protect your information is explained in the privacy notice at meetstudio.art/privacy.

10If terms are broken

If you break these terms, we may stop you from sending inquiries or requests, or close your account. Where reasonable, we will tell you why first.

For review: the website has no suspension feature today; closing an account means deleting it. Confirm the wording and whether a warning step is required.

11Deleting your account

  • You can ask us to delete your account from your account page. The M.E.E.T. team completes the deletion within 30 days and confirms by email.
  • Your login, your account details and the inquiries you sent are deleted.
  • If you sent project requests, the project record and its figures are kept for our business records, with your name replaced by "Deleted member".

For review: confirm that keeping project records this way is lawful, how long they may be kept, and how this must be described here and in the privacy notice.
