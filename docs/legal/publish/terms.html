Member terms

Member Terms of Use

Version 2026-10-26, published for information. These terms describe how M.E.E.T. works for Personal, Professional and Business & Organization accounts. M.E.E.T. is completing its company registration: once it is complete, the details of the company, the law that applies and a version of these terms for you to accept will be added, and we will tell you before anything changes for you.

1. About M.E.E.T.

M.E.E.T. (Mind Exploring Every Tradition) runs meetstudio.art, a platform for discovering artists and their work, writing to artists, and sending commission ideas, collaborations and project briefs to the M.E.E.T. team. Artists who join M.E.E.T. follow the separate artist terms. How we handle personal information is explained in the privacy notice.

2. Your account

  • You can create a Personal, Professional, or Business & Organization account. Everyone can browse, write to artists, send commission ideas and propose collaborations. Project briefs are for Professional and Business & Organization accounts.
  • Please give accurate details and keep them up to date. You can change most of them in your account. Your email address and account type cannot be changed there: write to us and the team changes them after checking the request.
  • You confirm your email address before you can sign in. A security check helps keep automated programs out at sign-up, sign-in and password reset.
  • Keep your password private and do not share your account. If you think someone else has used it, change your password, use "Sign out on all devices" in your account, and tell us.
  • If you register for a company, studio or organization, you confirm that you may act for it.

3. What M.E.E.T. offers

  • Discovery. The artists and artworks you see have been reviewed by the M.E.E.T. team before they appear. Review is not a guarantee of an artist's availability, prices, or the quality or authenticity of a work.
  • Inquiries. You can write to an artist about an artwork or a commission. The artist receives your name, account type and country, with your message, but not your email address or phone number. The artist can reply once through M.E.E.T. and decides whether to reply at all.
  • Requests to the team. Commission ideas, collaborations and project briefs go to the M.E.E.T. team. The team may review them, contact artists to check availability, pricing and timing, and come back to you with updates and proposals in your account. The team is not obliged to take on a request or to find an artist for it.
  • Service emails. Confirmations, copies of your inquiries, artists' replies and updates on your requests are part of the service.
  • Fair use. To protect artists and the platform, the number of inquiries and requests you can send in a day is limited.

4. Inquiries, proposals and agreements

  • An inquiry or a request is a first contact. It is not an order or an offer to buy, and it does not oblige you, an artist or M.E.E.T. to go ahead.
  • A proposal from the team sets out a creative direction, the artists and their contributions, an estimated budget and a timeline. Nothing goes ahead until you accept it in your account, and you can ask for changes or decline it instead.
  • Anything you arrange directly with an artist, such as a purchase or commission, is between you and that artist. M.E.E.T. is not part of it unless M.E.E.T. agrees in writing to provide a service.
  • If M.E.E.T. is to provide a paid service, such as sourcing or coordinating artists for a project, it will do so only under a separate written agreement made with you before the service begins, setting out what M.E.E.T. will do and any fees.
  • The statuses shown for your requests are for information. A status alone does not create or change an agreement.

5. Prices and payments

M.E.E.T. does not sell artworks and does not process payments. A price shown on an artwork is set by the artist, may change, and is for information only; it may not include taxes, delivery or other costs. Creating an account, browsing, writing to artists and sending requests are free. M.E.E.T. will only charge you a fee under a written agreement you have accepted.

6. Artworks and content

  • Artists keep the rights in their artworks and photographs. You may view them on M.E.E.T. and share links to M.E.E.T. pages.
  • Without the rights holder's permission, please do not copy, download in bulk, reproduce, sell or otherwise reuse artworks or photographs from M.E.E.T., and do not use them to train artificial-intelligence systems. This does not restrict any use the law allows without permission.
  • The M.E.E.T. name, logo, website design and texts belong to M.E.E.T.
  • You keep the rights in what you send us. You allow M.E.E.T. to store and use it as needed to run the platform, deliver your inquiries and work on your requests.
  • If you think something on M.E.E.T. infringes your rights, write to us with the page address and an explanation, and we will look into it.

7. Using M.E.E.T. respectfully

Please do not use M.E.E.T. to break the law, deceive, impersonate others, harass anyone, send spam or messages unrelated to an artist's work, collect information about artists or members for other purposes, scrape the website with automated tools, get around its security or limits, or interfere with how it works.

8. If these terms are not respected

If we reasonably believe an account is used against these terms, or puts artists, members or M.E.E.T. at risk, we may decline a request, remove content, or close the account. Where reasonable, we will tell you first and give you a chance to respond. If you disagree with a decision, write to us and we will consider it.

9. Deleting your account

Accounts are not deleted from the account page. If you would like your account deleted, write to us from your account's email address. The team handles the request and confirms by email what is deleted and what M.E.E.T. keeps, such as records of requests and projects. The privacy notice explains this in more detail.

10. Availability and changes

We work to keep M.E.E.T. available and secure, but it may sometimes be interrupted. We may add, change or remove features; when a change matters to how you use your account, we will tell you in advance where we reasonably can. We may update these terms, and will tell you about important changes before they take effect.

11. Contact

For anything about your account or these terms, write to minds.exploring.traditions@gmail.com.
