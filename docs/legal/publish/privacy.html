Privacy notice

How we look after your information

Version 2026-10-26. This notice covers the M.E.E.T. website, artist applications and studios, member accounts, and the requests members send to the M.E.E.T. team.

Who we are

M.E.E.T. (Mind Exploring Every Tradition) runs meetstudio.art. M.E.E.T. is completing its company registration; the name and registered details of the company responsible for your information will be added here once registration is complete. Until then, you can reach us at minds.exploring.traditions@gmail.com about anything in this notice.

When you visit the website

This site uses no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics. To keep you signed in, your browser stores your sign-in session. If you start a request form before signing in, your answers are kept in your browser until you send them, so they are not lost while you sign in.

When you sign up, sign in or reset your password, a security check run by our security provider confirms you are a person, not an automated program. It uses technical information from your browser and connection to do so. Our hosting provider processes the technical information needed to deliver the pages to you.

If you apply as an artist

We store what you type into the application form: your full name, professional or artist name, email address, country and city, your disciplines and description of your practice, any links you share, the interests you tick, where you found M.E.E.T. if you tell us, and the date you gave consent. The form asks for no uploads, passwords or payment details.

We use it to review your application, to contact you about it and about M.E.E.T. as it develops, and to understand which disciplines, places and interests are represented. We do so on the basis of the consent you give in the form, which you can withdraw at any time. Only the M.E.E.T. team can see your application. The team also keeps a working copy of applications in a private online spreadsheet that only the team can open.

If your application is approved

We create a M.E.E.T. studio for you. When the M.E.E.T. team approves your profile and your artworks, they become public: your artist name, city and country, disciplines, materials, techniques, biography, pictures and approved artworks, with the price of an artwork only if you choose to show it. Your legal name, email address, social media and website links are never shown publicly. Drafts and anything not approved stay private.

When a member writes to you, you receive their name, account type, country and message. When you write to the team from Support in your studio, we keep your message and our note on it.

If you create a member account

When you create a Personal, Professional or Business & Organization account, we store the details you enter: your full name, email address, country, phone number if you give it, your password in encrypted form, and, depending on the account type, your professional role, company or studio, business or organization name, business type, contact person and website. When you create the account, you agree that M.E.E.T. stores these details. We use them to run your account and the services you ask for, and to contact you about them. Only you and the M.E.E.T. team can see them, and they are not shown publicly.

You can change most of your details at any time in your account. Your email address and account type cannot be changed there: if you need either changed, write to us and the team changes it for you after checking the request. We record the change and confirm it to you by email.

When you write to an artist

The artist receives your name, account type and country, with your message, in their studio and by email. Your email address and phone number are not shared with the artist; an artist may choose to include their own contact details in their reply. You receive a copy of your inquiry by email, and the M.E.E.T. team receives an email copy of each new inquiry.

When you send a request to the M.E.E.T. team

Commission ideas, collaboration requests and project briefs go to the M.E.E.T. team, not directly to artists. We store what you send: your idea or brief, the type of artwork or project, budget, timing, inspiration, location, the artists you chose, and, for project briefs, any files you attach. Files are kept in private storage that only you and the team members handling requests can open.

To work on your request, the team records its own working information: the status of the request, the next steps, internal notes, which artists were contacted and what they answered, proposals, and, where relevant, agreed figures. Internal notes and the artists' answers are not shown to you. Figures are visible only to founders and to the team member your request is assigned to. To check availability, pricing and timing, the team may share the details of your request with the artists it contacts.

You see your request's progress, the updates the team shares with you and any proposal sent to you in your account, and we email you when a proposal or update is ready. When you accept, ask to change or decline a proposal, your answer is recorded and the team is told by email.

Emails

Emails from M.E.E.T. are sent from hello@meetstudio.art through our email delivery provider. Replies, and messages sent to hello@meetstudio.art, are forwarded by our email forwarding provider to the team's email inbox. The team's inbox also receives email copies of new applications, inquiries and requests, and answers to proposals. Every week, the founders receive by email a copy of the inquiry and request records, including members' contact details, so that the work can be recovered and continued.

The services we use

  • A database, sign-in and file storage provider, with servers in the European Union.
  • A website hosting provider, which also runs the functions that send emails.
  • A security provider, for the check at sign-up, sign-in and password reset.
  • An email delivery provider, for emails sent by M.E.E.T.
  • An email forwarding provider, for emails sent to hello@meetstudio.art.
  • An email and office software provider, for the team's inbox and the private spreadsheet with a copy of artist applications.

We never sell your information or share it with anyone for marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep artist applications while M.E.E.T. is being developed and for up to 24 months after, unless you ask us to delete yours sooner. We keep member accounts while they exist, and records of requests and projects as business records. We are defining the exact retention periods for member accounts, requests, email copies and records exports, and will publish them in this notice.

Deleting your account

Member accounts are not deleted from the account page. If you would like your account deleted, write to us from the email address of your account. The team handles the request and confirms by email what is deleted and what M.E.E.T. keeps. Today, deleting an account removes your login, your account details and the inquiries you sent from our records. Records of your requests are kept with your name removed; they still contain the other details you gave, such as your organization, country and description, so we continue to treat them as personal information. Copies already delivered to other mailboxes, such as an inquiry an artist received, cannot be recalled by us.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, at any time. Please write to minds.exploring.traditions@gmail.com and include your reference if you have one. We will reply within 30 days.

Applying is not a contract

Applying tells us you are interested. It does not guarantee acceptance, paid work, commissions or a collaboration.

Changes to this notice

We update this notice when the way we handle information changes, and show the date of the version above.
