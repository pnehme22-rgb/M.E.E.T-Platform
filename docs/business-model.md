# M.E.E.T. business model: approved principles (26 October 2026)

Recorded from the founder's decisions. These are principles for future implementation and agreements, not published prices. Nothing here is implemented as automatic calculation or payment.

## Status today

- Accounts and basic platform access are free. Artists pay no membership fee.
- The website takes no payments. Payments will not be activated before company registration.
- Company registration comes before artist onboarding and operational launch.
- Exact rates, calculation methods and legal or commercial terms are **undecided**.
- The team records figures by hand per request in the console (estimated value, agreed M.E.E.T. fee or commission, final value, final M.E.E.T. revenue, payment status). Only founders and the team member assigned to the request can see them (database rule, update 013).

## Revenue streams

M.E.E.T. may earn revenue through:

1. Commission on artwork sales.
2. Fees or commission on individual commissioned artworks.
3. Platform and connection fees.
4. Client sourcing, matching and coordination fees.
5. Sourcing objects, products, materials or suppliers for artists.
6. A percentage of collaborative artworks M.E.E.T. develops or enables.
7. Sales of M.E.E.T.-created collaborative artworks through a future products section.

Depending on the transaction and its written agreement, the artist, the client or both may pay the applicable fee.

## Rules for fees

- **Written and transparent:** every applicable fee is disclosed and agreed in writing before work begins.
- **Baseline platform fee:** M.E.E.T. earns a platform/connection fee on projects that result from connections made through the platform, even when the team does not manage the transaction afterwards.
- **One sourcing fee:** artist sourcing is quoted as a single number covering the platform, sourcing and matching, and the sales team's time. It is not split into separate line items.
- **When it is set:** the sourcing fee is established when the client and artist agree and the order is confirmed, not charged up front.
- **Involvement:** the more M.E.E.T. does (creative development, finding more artists, coordination, sourcing, project support), the higher the fee may be.
- **Sourcing for artists:** fees for finding objects, products, materials or suppliers reflect value, difficulty, effort and the request. No fixed universal rate.
- **Artist-referred clients:** a reduced fee, to encourage artists to bring existing clients to the platform. Returning clients pay the normal fee for new projects unless a written arrangement says otherwise.
- **Sales and individual commissions:** the commission basis and rate may differ between artwork sales and commissioned works. For commissioned works, the fee may rise with complexity and M.E.E.T.'s involvement.

## M.E.E.T.-created collaborative artworks

Collaboration is central to M.E.E.T.: it can enable original works that would not otherwise exist. A collaboration may be initiated by M.E.E.T., proposed by artists, or requested by a client (with M.E.E.T. developing the concept and connecting two or more artists).

- When M.E.E.T. develops or enables a qualifying multi-artist creation, it takes an agreed percentage of the project or artwork, whoever started the collaboration.
- These works enter the **M.E.E.T. Archive** and may be exhibited, promoted and sold through a future M.E.E.T.-created products section. Commercial use needs written agreements with the participating artists.
- Pricing depends on the project: M.E.E.T. may set the retail price for some works and agree it jointly with the artists for others.
- Artists' shares are based on contribution, not automatically equal, and are agreed project by project before work begins.
- M.E.E.T.'s percentage is separate from the artists' shares.

**Undecided:** the percentage, the calculation formula, ownership, intellectual property rights and detailed commercial terms.

## Deferred

- Exhibition costs (transport, installation, exhibition fees) and their effect on revenue distribution.
- Online payments, automatic commission calculation, published rates.
- The M.E.E.T. Archive and the products section (not built).
- Agreement templates for sales, commissions, sourcing and collaborations (to prepare with the lawyer after registration).
